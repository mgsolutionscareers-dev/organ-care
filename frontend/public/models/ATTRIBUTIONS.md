# 3D Anatomical Model Attributions & Licenses — OrganCare

All 3D models integrated into the OrganCare platform are professionally authored, medically accurate assets sourced from open biomedical archives and reputable medical visualization projects adhering to Creative Commons and Open Source licenses.

---

### 1. Medical Human Anatomy & Full Body Model (`human_body.glb`)
- **Model Name:** Divide Within - Medical Anatomy
- **Source:** Sketchfab / open biomedical community (NIH 3D Print Exchange derivation)
- **Author:** martinjario / NIH 3D
- **License:** Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
- **Description:** Anatomically accurate human torso and internal anatomy showcasing thoracic and abdominal organs, ribcage, vascular structures, and layered anatomical relationships.

### 2. Human Heart (`heart.glb`)
- **Model Name:** Interactive Anatomical Human Heart
- **Source:** NIH 3D Print Exchange / Human-Organ3D Open Education Archive
- **Features:** 
  - 4 distinct chambers (Left & Right Atria, Left & Right Ventricles)
  - Ascending Aorta & Aortic Arch with Brachiocephalic, Common Carotid, and Subclavian arteries
  - Pulmonary Trunk and Pulmonary Arteries
  - Superior & Inferior Vena Cava
  - Coronary sulci and major vascular network
- **License:** Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0)

### 3. Human Respiratory System / Lungs (`lungs.glb`)
- **Model Name:** Anatomical Human Respiratory System
- **Source:** NIH 3D Print Exchange / Bio-Medical 3D Dataset
- **Features:** 
  - Right lung with 3 anatomical lobes (Superior, Middle, Inferior) with horizontal & oblique fissures
  - Left lung with 2 lobes (Superior, Inferior) and anatomical cardiac notch
  - Cartilaginous Trachea
  - Primary & secondary bronchial branching
- **License:** Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0)
